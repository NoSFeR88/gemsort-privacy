Delete Your Data

Gem Sort does not have user accounts. All game data is stored locally on your device. No personal data is stored on our servers.

How to Delete Your Data

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it by:

  1. Clear App Data: Go to Settings > Apps > Gem Sort > Storage > Clear Data
  2. Uninstall the App: This removes all game data permanently
  3. Reset Advertising ID: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID (this dissociates your device from any ad profile)

Third-Party Data

Anonymous analytics data collected by Firebase Analytics is automatically deleted after 14 months per Google's retention policy. You cannot request deletion of this data individually as it is not linked to any personal identifier.
